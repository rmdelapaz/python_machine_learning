5.2

Bagging and Random Forests

Turn one jumpy tree into a steady crowd.

5.2 · Mental model

A panel of new hires

  • One diligent new hire memorizes every file: confident and quirky
  • Hire 100; each studies a random stack of files from the same archive
  • Average their opinions: the quirks point different ways and cancel
  • A forest: at each decision, only a random handful of columns may be used
WHY Variance of an average: ρσ² + (1 − ρ)σ²/n. More models, and less alike models.

5.2 · Common mistakes

Three you will meet

  • base_estimator=: TypeError. It is estimator=
  • oob_score=True with bootstrap=False: ValueError
  • Training AUC 1.000 reported as a result (OOB 0.851)
  • No random_state: different numbers every run
NOTE More trees never overfit: they cost time.

5.3

Gradient Boosting

Many small corrections.

5.3 · Mental model

Putting on a golf green

  • The first putt gets close; the next corrects what is left
  • Start from the average; each small tree aims at the remaining error
  • The learning rate: how hard you hit each putt
  • Forest: independent experts, averaged (variance). Boosting: corrections in sequence (bias)
WHY F = F + ν · tree: the whole algorithm.

5.3 · Common mistakes

Types and old tutorials

  • Text columns: could not convert string to float
  • XGBoost: early_stopping_rounds in fit(): TypeError
  • LightGBM: eval_set deprecated: eval_X, eval_y
  • Early stopping on the TEST set: no error, inflated score
RULE The validation set comes from the training rows. Always.

5.4

Voting and Stacking

A crowd is wise only when its members disagree.

5.4 · Mental model

The gear-testing panel

  • Three testers, each right 70% of the time
  • Independent: the majority is right more often than any one
  • All read the same review: the panel is no better than the review
  • Voting counts hands; stacking learns whom to trust
WHY Is the gain larger than the noise, and worth the moving parts?
5.4 · Simulation

More voters help only if they disagree

shared 0.0:  1 voter 0.695   5 voters 0.837   25 voters 0.982
shared 0.5:  1 voter 0.704   5 voters 0.754   25 voters 0.721
shared 0.9:  1 voter 0.703   5 voters 0.699   25 voters 0.702
WHY Five independent 70% voters: 0.837. Ninety percent copying: no better than one.

5.4 · Voting

Soft vote: an average of probabilities

average of all three   ROC AUC 0.863
average of forest + boost  ROC AUC 0.865
average of logreg + boost  ROC AUC 0.858
average of logreg + forest ROC AUC 0.853
  • The team edges past its best member by thousandths
  • Logistic + boost (0.858) loses to boost alone (0.862)
  • Hard voting has no predict_proba: no ranking, no threshold
WATCH An equal vote lets a weak member drag the team down.

5.4 · Stacking

A manager who learns whom to trust

  1. Out-of-fold predictions for every training row (5 folds)
  2. A simple meta-model learns weights from them
  3. Members refit on all training rows
  4. New rows: member predictions → meta-model → final prediction
WHY StackingClassifier(estimators=members, final_estimator=LogisticRegression(), cv=cv)
5.4 · Code

StackingClassifier: weights and score

stack = StackingClassifier(estimators=members,
                           final_estimator=LogisticRegression(), cv=cv)
stack.fit(X_train, y_train)
print(stack.final_estimator_.coef_[0].round(2))
print(round(roc_auc_score(y_test, stack.predict_proba(X_test)[:, 1]), 3))
[1.03 5.23 1.55]
0.864
WHY Weights describe how opinions combine. They say nothing about why customers churn.
5.4 · Why out-of-fold?

Members look brilliant on rows they trained on

logreg  training-row ROC AUC: in-sample 0.842   out-of-fold 0.832
forest  training-row ROC AUC: in-sample 0.981   out-of-fold 0.867
boost   training-row ROC AUC: in-sample 0.999   out-of-fold 0.859
WATCH A meta-model trained on in-sample predictions learns: trust the most overconfident member.

5.4 · Common mistakes

What goes wrong with ensembles

  • Members without names: ValueError
  • predict_proba on a hard vote: AttributeError
  • TimeSeriesSplit as stacking cv: not a partition
  • Weights or diversity measured on the test set
  • Meta-model on in-sample predictions: silent

Model Selection and Learning Curves

Distrust a single number.

6.1 · Mental model

Testing tents

  • Same campsites (folds), same nights (repeats)
  • Compare night by night, not one storm against one calm
  • Which is best? same pipeline, same folds, one metric chosen first
  • Is the gap real? paired differences
  • What would help? validation and learning curves

6.1 · Common mistakes

Two loud, two silent

  • scoring="auc": no such scorer; roc_auc
  • param_name="max_depth" on a pipeline: add the step name
  • A splitter per model: 9 wins of 10 become 6 of 10
  • Crowning a winner by the third decimal
WATCH Say "within noise" when it is.

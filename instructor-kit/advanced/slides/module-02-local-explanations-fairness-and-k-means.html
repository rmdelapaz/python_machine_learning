7.3 · Fairness

Which harm? Which metric?

  • Flag rate by group: demographic parity
  • Recall among each group's churners: equal opportunity
  • Precision among each group's flagged: wasted offers
  • The model never sees age: that guarantees nothing
  • Age is used only to audit, never as an input
WHY A churner who misses an offer is the harm here: compare recall.

7.3 · Common mistakes

Messages that do not say what is wrong

  • Gaps in LIME's data: ValueError: Domain error ... truncnorm
  • Labels, not probabilities: NotImplementedError
  • A pandas row: KeyError: 0. Use .to_numpy()
  • 100 or 300 variants: reasons that change with the seed
  • "We removed age, so it is fair"

8.1

K-Means Clustering

Campfires on a big campground.

8.1 · Mental model

Campfires on a big campground

  • k fires lit at random spots
  • Assign: every hiker walks to the nearest fire
  • Move: every fire moves to the middle of its crowd
  • Repeat until nobody switches
  • Inertia = total squared distance to the own fire: each round lowers it
RULE K-Means always returns k groups, even when there are none. Checking is your job.

8.1 · Common mistakes

Two errors and three silent traps

  • A text column: could not convert string to float
  • Gaps: Input X contains NaN
  • Unscaled columns: the biggest numbers decide
  • Scaled centers read as real values: use inverse_transform
  • "Cluster 2 is premium": numbers change between runs

8.2 · Mental model

Sorting the returns pile

  • Cohesion: things in the same bin are alike
  • Separation: different bins are clearly different
  • Stability: a colleague would sort them much the same way
  • Usefulness: the bins help whoever unpacks them
EVIDENCE Elbow · silhouette · stability · usefulness. Look for agreement.

8.2 · Silhouette

Tight inside, far apart

  • a = average distance to the point's own cluster
  • b = average distance to the nearest other cluster
  • s = (b − a) / max(a, b): near 1 snug · near 0 border · negative misplaced
  • Look per cluster, not just the mean (35 customers negative here)
  • Every pairwise distance: sample on big data

8.2 · Common mistakes

Three traps

  • k by the lowest inertia: always the largest k (9 here)
  • A silhouette for k = 1: ValueError: Number of labels is 1
  • Silhouettes compared across different feature sets
  • A scaler refitted on new customers: put everything in one pipeline

8.3

Hierarchical Clustering

Every level of grouping at once.

8.3 · Mental model

Tidying the gear closet

  • Every item starts on its own
  • Box the two closest things together; write down how far apart they were
  • Repeat until everything is in one box: n − 1 merges
  • The dendrogram draws the merges; a cut gives flat clusters
WHY A long empty stretch in the tree is a natural number of clusters.

8.3 · Linkage

Distance between two groups is a choice

  • Single: closest pair · follows chains · one bridge glues groups
  • Complete: farthest pair · compact · outlier-sensitive
  • Average: all pairs · works with any distance
  • Ward: increase in variance · round groups · Euclidean only

8.3 · Common mistakes

Older tutorials, renamed parameters

  • affinity=: TypeError. Use metric=
  • Ward with cosine: ValueError. Use average linkage
  • Both n_clusters and distance_threshold: ValueError
  • No predict: nearest mean, or a classifier
  • Single linkage on noisy customers: 5,605 in one cluster

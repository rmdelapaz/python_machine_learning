Is the gain real?

# paired bootstrap: item-kNN vs best sellers, same customers
item-kNN minus popularity: 0.099 (95% CI 0.082 to 0.116)
      customers  popularity  item_kNN
2-4         607       0.199     0.257
5-8         987       0.202     0.280
9-15        950       0.204     0.334
16+         456       0.246     0.382
WHY An interval far from zero, and a gain in every history group.
Milestone 6 · Intervals

Two good models, one test month

  • Fraud, June: 64 frauds only
  • Average precision intervals are about 0.25 wide
  • A paired difference that includes 0: cannot rank them
  • Then choose on other grounds, and say which
WATCH Resample weeks or customers when rows are not independent.
Milestone 6 · Subgroups

Read errors with their baseline

  • Demand 2025, MAE in units: baseline → model
  • Footwear 18.7 → 12.5 (sells about 46 a week)
  • Snow 13.3 → 5.8 (sells about 12 a week)
  • Absolute errors follow volume: compare like with like

An interval that includes zero

import numpy as np
diffs = np.array([0.02, -0.01, 0.03, 0.00, 0.05])
print(round(float(diffs.mean()), 3), bool(np.percentile(diffs, 2.5) < 0))

Milestone 7 · Who bears the errors

A finding is a design input

  • New accounts: 8% of legitimate payments, about 29% of false alarms
  • A held payment is a real customer kept waiting
  • So: human review, a fast release path, results by account age

Milestone 8 · Weak and strong

Say it the strong way

  • Weak: "The model is 99.1% accurate."
  • Strong: "Holding the riskiest 1% catches about half of June's fraud; doing nothing catches none."
  • Weak: "More data is needed."
  • Strong: "Only 64 June frauds: two good models cannot be ranked."

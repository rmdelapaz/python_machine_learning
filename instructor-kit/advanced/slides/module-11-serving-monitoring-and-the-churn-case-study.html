12.2 · Monitoring

What can you watch before the labels arrive?

  • Input drift: PSI per feature (no labels)
  • Data quality: missing rates, 422 rejections (no labels)
  • Prediction drift: mean score, offer rate (no labels)
  • Performance: AUC, offer precision (labels, months later)
WHY PSI: below 0.1 stable · 0.1 to 0.25 watch · above 0.25 shift. Chosen before you look.

12.2 · Drift is a question

Three changes, three answers

  • Broken email tracker: data bug. Fix the feed; never retrain on it
  • Delivery delay: real change. Higher scores may be right; someone decides the budget
  • Store-wide sale: concept drift? Only labels can confirm
PLAN Log every prediction with inputs, version and time: the label check needs it.
12.2 · Common mistakes

Wrong numbers, status 200

  • frame.to_dict("records") with gaps: NaN is not JSON
  • A missing column: callers must never build the table
  • The frame without the training dtypes: silent skew
  • pydantic 1 habits: .dict() → model_dump()

Milestone 2 · Example

A feature-timing table (fraud)

  • amount, category, items: known at checkout · yes
  • account_age_days, device_new: known at checkout · yes
  • velocity_24h: known IF the counter updates in real time · check
  • transaction_id: a label · never
  • timestamp: how you split, not a feature · never
Milestone 2 · Quirks

Odd values to look for

  • Demand: zeros that are stockouts, not zero demand
  • Fraud: the fraud rate drifts month to month
  • Reviews: stars that contradict the text; 3-star reviews
  • Recs: products nobody bought yet; brand-new customers
  • Segments: skewed columns; one column in dollars

Milestone 3 · The harness

One function for every candidate

  • Demand: rolling folds over 2024
  • Fraud: train January to April, validate on May
  • Reviews: stratified folds, accuracy AND negative recall
  • Recs: a second hidden purchase from the training rows
  • Segments: silhouette AND stability across seeds
FIRST The baseline goes through it first.
Studio I · Milestone 4

The simplest sensible pipeline

  • One-hot + ridge · scaled + logistic regression
  • TF-IDF + logistic regression · item-kNN · K-Means on scaled features
  • Through your harness, next to the baseline
  • Loses? Look for a bug before a better model
Milestone 4 · The log

Every attempt, one line

  • Candidate · CV score · spread across folds · one line of why
  • Keep a change only if it beats the best by more than the spread
  • Features first, then a stronger model, then modest tuning
  • Deep learning only if it beats tuned boosting or a linear model

Common mistakes

Quietly wrong from the first hour

  • A random split on time-ordered data
  • A feature known only after the prediction moment
  • Choosing k or a threshold on the test set
  • Accuracy on a rare event
  • Scaling outside the pipeline

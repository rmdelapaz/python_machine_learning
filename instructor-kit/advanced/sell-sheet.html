What You Get

For every session (×13)
  • Facilitator guide: a 0:00–3:00 agenda, scripted live demos with real output, the mistakes to expect, what to cut if you run long
  • Participant workbook: key ideas, worked examples, predict-then-run items, a 12-item self-check
  • Answer key: every item explained, including why each wrong option is wrong
  • Slide deck and labs: new starter and solution programs on the course data
Across the kit
  • Combined, print-ready books: Facilitator Guide, Participant Workbook, Answer Key
  • Slide decks (401 slides) and runnable labs (50 starter/solution pairs, 18 find-the-bug handouts), verified by a one-command checker that runs offline
  • Checkpoint 1 (Session 6), a two-session capstone with five problems and a final assessment rubric, a setup guide and a public syllabus

Why Instructors Choose It

  • Pairs with Machine Learning Foundations. The companion kit (11 sessions) teaches the supervised core; this one picks up where it ends: 24 sessions, 72 hours together, or each on its own.
  • One dataset, start to finish. Pinecrest Outfitters, a fictional retailer: churn, demand, fraud, reviews, purchases, store sales; Fashion-MNIST and EuroSAT for images.
  • Deep learning on a laptop CPU. Every lab runs in under 90 seconds, slow steps ship as small fixtures, and each deep-learning session has a CPU-time plan. No GPU.
  • Honest ML. Baselines first, the test set opened once, fairness and drift checks, negative results reported as results.
  • For trainers, bootcamps, colleges and CE, corporate L&D and workforce programs; weekly, twice weekly or intensive.
